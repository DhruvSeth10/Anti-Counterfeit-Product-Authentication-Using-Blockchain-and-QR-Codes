Anti-Counterfeit Product Authentication

Prototype simulation of the ProductAuth smart contract (same rules as contracts/ProductAuth.sol). Switch the acting wallet, register a product, pass it down the chain, then try to verify or clone it.

1 · Register product (Manufacturer)

2 · Transfer custody (current owner)

3 · Verify a QR (consumer / anyone)

Clone test: sell a product to Consumer A, then switch to Consumer B and scan the same QR.
